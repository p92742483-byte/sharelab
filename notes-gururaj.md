# ResourceShare – Unlock Underused Equipment and Spaces

## 📌 Project Overview

ResourceShare is a resource-sharing platform designed to help colleges, laboratories, makerspaces, community organisations, and small businesses share underused equipment, facilities, and technical resources with people who need them.

The platform aims to make resource access more affordable, convenient, safe, and transparent by allowing users to discover available resources and request access while enabling owners to maintain control over their property.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many institutions own valuable resources such as laboratories, classrooms, computers, cameras, testing devices, and workshop equipment that remain unused for long periods. Meanwhile, students, researchers, creators, and startups may not be able to afford purchasing or renting these resources.

Traditional booking systems often lack proper eligibility checks, approval workflows, condition tracking, and accountability.

ResourceShare aims to address these challenges through a structured and trustworthy resource-sharing experience.

## ✨ Key Platform Features

The proposed platform is designed around the following capabilities:

- **Resource Discovery:** Browse available equipment, spaces, and facilities.
- **Resource Details:** View availability, location, eligibility requirements, and usage conditions.
- **Booking Requests:** Request access to a resource.
- **Owner Approval:** Support approval, rejection, and waiting-list workflows.
- **Eligibility and Safety:** Communicate training, qualification, supervision, and authorisation requirements.
- **Check-in and Check-out:** Track resource usage and return.
- **Condition Reporting:** Record the condition of resources before and after use.
- **Incident Management:** Support reporting of damage, delays, and other issues.
- **Fair Resource Allocation:** Help prevent repeated reservations from monopolising limited resources.
- **Activity History:** Maintain records of important actions for accountability.

## 🔄 User Workflow

1. An owner lists an available resource.
2. A user discovers the resource and reviews its details.
3. The user checks the eligibility and usage requirements.
4. The user submits a booking request.
5. The owner or authorised manager reviews the request.
6. The request is approved, rejected, or waitlisted.
7. The user checks in and records the initial condition.
8. The resource is returned and its final condition is recorded.
9. Any damage, delay, or conflict can be reported.
10. The transaction history provides a record of the resource-sharing process.

## 🌍 Impact

ResourceShare aims to improve access to expensive equipment and facilities, reduce unnecessary purchases, encourage better utilisation of existing resources, and promote safe and accountable sharing among institutions and communities.
