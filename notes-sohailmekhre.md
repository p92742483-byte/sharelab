# ResourceShare – Unlock Underused Equipment and Spaces

## 📌 Project Overview

ResourceShare is a resource-sharing platform designed to connect people who need equipment, facilities, and technical resources with institutions and communities that have underutilised resources.

The platform focuses on making resource sharing convenient, accessible, safe, and transparent for colleges, laboratories, makerspaces, community organisations, and small businesses.

It aims to simplify resource discovery, booking requests, eligibility checks, approvals, and resource-return tracking through a user-friendly digital experience.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many institutions have valuable resources such as laboratories, classrooms, computers, cameras, and specialised equipment that remain unused for long periods. At the same time, students, researchers, creators, and startups may struggle to afford these resources.

Existing booking systems may not adequately address usability, eligibility requirements, safety, approval processes, and accountability.

ResourceShare aims to bridge this gap by providing a clear and intuitive interface for discovering, requesting, and sharing resources responsibly.

## ✨ Design Goals

- **Simplicity:** Make the platform easy to understand, even for first-time users.
- **Accessibility:** Present information clearly with readable text and intuitive navigation.
- **Consistency:** Maintain consistent layouts, typography, spacing, and visual elements.
- **Transparency:** Make resource availability, eligibility, and booking status easy to understand.
- **Trust:** Communicate resource rules, approval decisions, and usage conditions clearly.
- **Efficiency:** Help users complete important tasks with fewer unnecessary steps.

## 🧭 User Experience Workflow

The design focuses on making the resource-sharing journey clear:

1. Users explore available resources.
2. Users review resource details and availability.
3. Users understand eligibility requirements and usage rules.
4. Users submit a booking request.
5. Users receive a clear indication of the request status.
6. Owners review and manage booking requests.
7. Users follow the check-in and return process.
8. Users can understand how to report damage, delays, or other issues.
